Tap a cell, then a number. Fill every row, column and diagonal to the same total.

The secret structure

The middle box is the heart: target ÷ 3. Opposite boxes always add to twice the centre — for centre 5 that's 10: 1+9, 2+8, 3+7, 4+6.

Count-down method

Place 1 in the top middle. Count three down, place the next number to the right. Blocked? Drop straight below instead. Repeat to 9.

Eight twins

Rotate or mirror any solved square — all 8 versions stay magic. The numbers move, the relationships never do.
