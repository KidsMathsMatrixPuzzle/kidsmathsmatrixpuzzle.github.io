📐 How Multiply Magic works

Every line (row, column, diagonal) has the same product — you multiply the three numbers, not add them.

Target = center³ (center × center × center). Set the center box and the goal appears in orange.

Opposite boxes through the center multiply to center².

Worked examples (center → target):

center 6 → 216 3 36 2 4 6 9 18 1 12
center 12 → 1728 6 72 4 8 12 18 36 2 24
center 16 → 4096 8 256 2 4 16 64 128 1 32

Tip: these are the same magic squares as the adding ones — written as powers. Maths has no boundary. 🙌

🔢 Three Types — how the numbers rise

Multiply Magic works with any family of numbers whose factors line up. Three ready examples, easy → hard:

  1. Type 1 · 2s & 3s → Target 216 (center 6)
    1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36 — built by multiplying 2s and 3s.
  2. Type 2 · bigger 2s & 3s → Target 1728 (center 12)
    2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 18, 24, 36, 72 — the same idea, scaled up.
  3. Type 3 · powers of 2 → Target 4096 (center 16)
    1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256 — each number doubles the last (2⁰ … 2⁸).

How the sequence rises: pick a center, then its factors are spread around it so every line multiplies back to center³. A bigger center means a bigger target and faster-rising numbers.

When / after: try them in order — 216 → 1728 → 4096 (small numbers first). Attempt these only after you are comfortable with the adding 5×5 — this is the advanced magic. 🧙
