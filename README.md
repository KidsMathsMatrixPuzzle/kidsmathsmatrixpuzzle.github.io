MathMatrix Pro++ is a browser-based puzzle collection with number, logic, maze, and traditional games. Open it on a phone, tablet, or computer. No account is needed.

## Puzzles

- **Magic Squares:** 3×3, 4×4, 5×5, 6×6, 8×8, and 10×10
- **3³ Multiply Magic:** rotate or mirror solutions to explore eight puzzle twins
- **Criss-Cross:** a guided math puzzle
- **Tunnels of SaNa Maze:** trace the tunnel that reaches the finish
- **Sir Ramanujan MathMagic:** birthday-number puzzle
- **7 Logic Gates:** AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR, XOR, and XNOR
- **Guess My Number:** Junior (1–99) and Advance (1–255)
- **Slide Magic:** Slide 1–15, Slide A–Z, and Slide A–Z + 1–9
- **Sudoku:** 3×3, 4×4, and 9×9 puzzles
- **Pallanguzhi**
- **Thayam:** 5×5 and 7×7

## Play and install

Play in a browser at [kidsmathsmatrixpuzzle.github.io](https://kidsmathsmatrixpuzzle.github.io/). To open the same link on another device, use the QR code on the app’s **Get App** page or choose **Share** or **Copy Link**.

You can add the app to a device’s home screen:

- **iPhone or iPad:** Open the game in Safari, tap **Share**, choose **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
- **Android:** Open the game in Chrome, open the **⋮** menu, then choose **Install App** or **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Windows or Mac:** In Chrome or Edge, use the install icon beside the address bar. Other browsers offer a bookmark or an add-to-home-screen option; the wording varies.

## Offline play

Open the app while online first. It saves the game files in the browser’s local app cache so you can play offline later. Clearing browser or site data can remove those files even if the home-screen icon remains. If the app then says **“You are offline,”** reopen it while online to rebuild its offline copy.
