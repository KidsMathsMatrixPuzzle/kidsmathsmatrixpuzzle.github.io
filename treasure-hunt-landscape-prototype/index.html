🗺️ Cave maze cheat sheet

Follow the fixed cave route through ten checkpoints. Room markers stay hidden during play; the tracker fills as SaNa reaches each checkpoint.

Play in order

  1. Start: choose a player color and press START to enter the cave.
  2. Key: collect it to open the Map locker.
  3. Map: collect and read it.
  4. Leaf Shield: collect it before the Tribes.
  5. Tribes: use the Leaf Shield on the actual crossing.
  6. Metal Mesh: collect it before the Dragon.
  7. Dragon: protect the whole party with the mesh on the actual crossing.
  8. Treasure and Rest Room: after collecting Treasure, keep to the main tunnel. Drink and sleep appear only after SaNa reaches the circled Rest Room passage. Crossing the Rest decoy shows the tired explorers and a red X, blocks forward movement, and requires retracing the trail to the Rest Room.
  9. Exit: follow the tunnel to finish the maze.

Hazards and detours

  • Key, Map and Treasure detours stay blocked until you collect the matching item.
  • Crossing the Tribes without the Leaf Shield sends SaNa back to Start and clears that attempt. The same color can try again. With the shield, the decoy crossing is blocked; turn back and cross the actual Tribes path.
  • At the Dragon, the Metal Mesh must cover the whole party. Without it, poison gas ends the run and retires that color; choose another color. With the mesh, the decoy crossing is blocked; turn back and cross the actual Dragon path safely.
  • The distant chest appears before the Treasure checkpoint; the explorers celebrate only after collecting it. Follow the main route to the circled Rest Room passage for the drink and sleep scenes. Crossing the Rest decoy shows the tired explorers, displays a red X, and blocks forward movement; retrace the trail to the Rest Room. Rest is a timed checkpoint: its tracker tick appears after the full rest timer.

Move SaNa

  • Hold one direction button or arrow key at a time; release at turns. You can also drag along the tunnel.
  • Keep to one continuous trail. To go back, retrace the trail from SaNa in reverse order. You cannot cross an old segment or jump across an intersection.

WATCH follows the route from the cave entrance and pauses at story messages. Press STOP to end it. CLEAR stops Watch, closes the story popup, clears progress and releases retired colors, then returns the game to pawn selection. Choose a color and press START for a new attempt.
🔊 Sound & Music

Choose how SaNa’s cave sounds.

Sound is the master switch for cave effects and background music. Turning it OFF stops both. Music controls only the background tune; turning it OFF leaves cave effects on.
