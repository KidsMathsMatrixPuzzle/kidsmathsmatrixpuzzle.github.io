💡 Tic-Tac-Toe Logic

3×3: get 3 in a row. Two players take turns on the same screen.

  1. X starts. Tap an empty square to place your mark, then pass to O.
  2. Complete a whole row, column, or diagonal to win. A 4×4 board needs 4 in a row; a 5×5 board needs 5.
  3. Look for your next winning move, and block your friend’s line before it is complete.
  4. If the board fills with no winning line, the round is a draw.

Watch varies the winning row, column, or diagonal and the order of moves, without adding to the scores. Tap Stop to return to your round.

New starts an empty round and keeps the scores. Clear empties the board and resets all scores. Changing size starts a fresh set of scores.

🔊 Sound & Music

Sound is the master switch for move tones and music. Turn Music off to keep only the move tones.
