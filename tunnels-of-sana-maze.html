💡 How to play

Two tunnels leave Start. One winds all the way to Finish; the other gets close, then ends at a wall. Follow the turns and use your own map in your mind.

  • Drag the colored marker one cell at a time. Walls stop it.
  • Drag back to the previous cell to retrace your route.
  • Choose New for another maze, Clear to restart this one, or Watch for a demonstration.

🔊 Sound & Music

Tap a button below to change it.

🔊 Sound is the master switch. Turning it OFF stops both the maze music and maze tones.

🎵 Music controls only the monkey background tune. Turning Music OFF keeps the maze tones working while Sound remains ON.
